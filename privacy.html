Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

repfind ("we", "us", or "our") operates the repfind mobile application and website at repfind.ca (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Search queries: We log the search terms you enter to improve search relevance and product discovery. These are stored anonymously and are not linked to your personal identity.

Usage data: We collect anonymous analytics such as page views, taps, and interactions to understand how the Service is used and to improve it.

Account data: If you create an account, we store your email address and saved product lists ("My Haul"). You can delete your account and data at any time.

Currency preference: Your selected display currency (USD, CAD, AUD, EUR, CNY) is stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve search results and product recommendations
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve the Service
  • To maintain your saved products and preferences
  • To prevent abuse and ensure Service integrity

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not process payments. All purchases are made directly with third-party shipping agents (KakoBuy, CNFans, OopBuy).
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location data, or contact lists.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The Service links to third-party shipping agents for product purchases. These services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their data practices.

We use Supabase for anonymous analytics storage and Google Tag Manager for analytics infrastructure.

Data Storage

Search analytics are stored on secure servers (Supabase). Your saved products and preferences are stored locally on your device unless you create an account.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. You may clear locally stored data by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy within the Service.
